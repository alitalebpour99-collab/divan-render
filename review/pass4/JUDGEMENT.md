## What changed (engine, not only the samples)
- **Column ships** are drawn at 0.4 (about 108 px, was 65 px). The strait's hook and «یک هفته» beat look at the
  outbound lane east of the narrows (span 2.8), the only stretch where six whole tankers of that size stay on open
  water. All six are drawn on every frame from 1.0 s to 9.3 s.
- **Header and marks land together.** Into a new beat the camera whips (6 frames, a dive 14) and the header, its
  things, its discs and their legend rows land as it arrives. A column on the Reel's clock never blinks out.
- **The measure** is a beat's news: it comes on the beat's first shot with its header.
- **Focus shots.** Once every disc of a beat is on the map, a shot that would only add a place name turns to the discs
  in turn: the camera leans to disc ①, then ②, the disc gets an amber halo and its legend row lights while the other
  rows step back. The beat's main thing stays in frame.
- **Reading time** no longer counts place names (glanced, not read), and a thing standing by its disc is looked at
  once with it. This shortens beats that only grew from filler names.
- **Discs off a line's ends.** A disc that would sit on a pipeline's end circle moves to the side of the line or beyond
  its end (on land for a land place, on clear water for a sea event).
- **The oil stops.** On a beat whose line gives a share of output lost, the pipeline's flow slows to a stop and drains
  on the shot after the share's row lands.
- The mine's room is as wide as the mine is drawn (its horns touched disc ① for five frames once the camera settled).
- Abqaiq sample: shorter legend rows («ابقیق، فرآوری نفت», «راس تنوره، پایانهٔ صادرات»), the share line «نیمی از
  تولید عربستان», and a shorter pipeline line for the last beat.
